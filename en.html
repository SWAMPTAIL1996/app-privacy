Privacy Policy

SWAMPTAIL (the “Developer”) handles users' information in the apps listed below in accordance with this policy.

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Apps covered by this policy

This policy applies to the apps listed in this table. When another app is added, its information handling practices will be added to this page.

AppInformation handling
ColoCollect (カラコレクト)
com.colocollect.game		Scores, play dates and times, event discovery and completion progress, and language, audio and control settings are stored on your device. The app has no feature that sends this information to the Developer's servers. It does not include advertising, analytics or account registration features.

2. Information used by the app and its purposes

ColoCollect has no feature that collects and sends personal information, such as your name, email address, location, contacts or advertising identifiers, to the Developer. Game records and settings stored on your device are used to continue your progress, display local rankings and retain your preferences.

3. Information provided when you contact us

If you contact the Developer by email, the Developer receives your email address, message and any information you choose to provide. This information is used to respond to your inquiry and communicate with you as necessary. You do not need to send passwords or other secrets.

4. Sharing with third parties

ColoCollect has no feature that sends game records or settings to third parties on behalf of the Developer. Personal information received through inquiries will not be provided to third parties without your consent, except where required by law. The Developer does not sell personal information.

5. Retention, deletion and security

Game records and settings remain on your device until you clear the app's storage through your device settings. Clearing storage also removes your scores and progress. If operating system backup or restoration is enabled, its handling is governed by your device settings and the service provider's policies.

Inquiry information is retained for as long as needed to respond and perform related follow-up, and information that is no longer needed is deleted. The Developer uses appropriate safeguards, such as access restrictions, to prevent unauthorized access and disclosure. To request deletion of information provided in an inquiry, please use the contact details below.

6. Children's information

ColoCollect has no feature that sends users' personal information to the Developer, regardless of their age. If the Developer becomes aware that personal information about a child has been provided through an inquiry or similar communication, appropriate action, including deletion where necessary, will be taken.

7. Visiting this page

When you visit this page, the website hosting provider may process information such as your IP address and access time to provide and secure its services. This is separate from transmitting game data from the app. For details, please see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the covered apps' features or information handling practices change. Where necessary, important changes will be announced in the app or through other appropriate means. You can review changes on this page and check the last updated date.
